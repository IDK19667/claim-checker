About Evident

Evident checks a health claim against the published research and shows its working.

Who makes it

Evident is an independent project made and run by Dhruva. It shows no ads, sells nothing and has no accounts, so no verdict makes it money.

What it does

You paste a claim. Evident searches PubMed, reads the abstracts of the closest studies, and writes a verdict that cites only studies it was shown. Every verdict says what is still open. "It's complicated" and "Not enough evidence" are ordinary answers.

What it does not do

It does not diagnose, treat or advise. It can misread an abstract, and PubMed does not index everything. Read the studies it lists, and take your own health questions to a clinician.

Methodology

How a check works, step by step, and the rules the code enforces on every verdict.

The steps

Your claim becomes a search. An AI model ({{ provider }}) turns the claim into the terms researchers use.

PubMed answers. Evident takes up to 8 of the closest matches, with their abstracts.

The studies get weighed, not counted. A meta-analysis of many trials outranks one small pilot. A case report about one patient barely counts.

The verdict is checked by code. A verdict can cite only studies it was shown. One with no citations, or resting only on case reports or animal and lab studies, becomes "Not enough evidence".

Every verdict says what is still open. The last line names the gap: who the studies left out, how short they ran, or what finding would change the answer.

Limits

The model can misread an abstract, and PubMed does not index everything. Strong evidence of a small effect is not strong evidence for a cure.
