This site shows no ads, has no accounts and sells no data. It keeps no record of who you are, so there is no data about you to sell. Each verdict is written by an outside AI service, which is sent the text of the claim and the published details of the studies, such as their titles and abstracts, and nothing about you.

What it will not do

It will not claim more certainty than the evidence gives. A verdict may cite only studies the model was shown, and a verdict that cites none becomes “{{ labels.insufficient }}” in the code, with one exception: on a few claims where a wrong answer can hurt someone, plain advice from major health bodies can decide the verdict without a study.
