Corrections

When Evident gets an answer wrong, here is how to say so and what happens to your report.

Report a wrong answer

Under every result there is a button, Report a wrong answer. You can add a short note saying what is wrong. You do not need to give a name or an email, and there is no account to sign in to.

What a report holds

The claim, the verdict it got, your note if you wrote one, and the date and time of the report. Nothing else: no name, no address, no cookie. Leave anything personal out of the note.

What happens to it

Reports are stored with the claim, so a wrong answer can be found and checked again. A report is anonymous, so there is no way to reply to it.

A saved answer is not kept forever: when someone checks the same claim more than a day later, it is checked against PubMed again, so a newer study can change it.

Not medical advice. This tool summarizes published research and can be wrong. For your own health, talk to a clinician.
